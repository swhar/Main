# /briefing — Start-of-Day Briefing

Run this at the start of each day to orient yourself and hit the ground running.

## Steps

1. Read `memory/profile.md` to recall current goals and focus areas.
2. Read `memory/tasks.md` to see what's active, in progress, and pending.
3. Read `memory/decisions.md` (last 3 entries) for any open decisions.
4. Ask: "Anything specific on your mind today, or any new context I should know before the briefing?"
5. Produce a structured briefing in this format:

---

## Today's Briefing — [Date]

**Top 3 Priorities**
1. [Most important task or goal today]
2. [Second priority]
3. [Third priority]

**Open Decisions**
- [Any decision that's pending and needs attention]

**Momentum Check**
- [What's been moving well lately]
- [What might be stalling or needs a nudge]

**Focus Recommendation**
> [One sentence: where to put your best energy today and why]

**Quick Wins Available**
- [1–2 small tasks that could be knocked out quickly for momentum]

---

6. Ask: "Want me to turn this into a full day plan? Use `/plan-day`."
